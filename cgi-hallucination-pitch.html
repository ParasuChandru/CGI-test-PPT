AI Assurance Initiative

A four-layer framework to eliminate hallucinations in client-facing AI.

Hallucinations are a solved engineering problem — when you invest in the right controls. Here's the stack we propose to deploy across BU workflows.

LAYER 1
Ground in Sources
Retrieval-Augmented Generation forces the model to cite verified internal documents — not invent facts.
LAYER 2
Automated Guardrails
Citation validation, schema checks, and fact-scoring run on every output before it reaches a human.
LAYER 3
Human Review Gates
Risk-tiered approval: light-touch for internal use, mandatory sign-off for client and regulated deliverables.
LAYER 4
Continuous Evaluation
Hallucination-rate dashboards per workflow, with monthly drift monitoring and prompt regression tests.
